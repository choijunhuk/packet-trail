MidnightAccess original soundtrack and sound effects
Production version: 2026-11-05 rhythmic soundtrack / circular-loop production upgrade

These audio assets and their note scores were produced specifically for the
Packet Trail / MidnightAccess project using original offline synthesis authored
by Codex (gpt-6.1-sol, high). No third-party music, samples, recordings or
generative music services were used. No external sample attribution or music
service subscription is required by this production pipeline.

The seven rhythmic score arrangements and the A07 ending edit use original
authored notes, harmonics and synthesized percussion. Looping music and rain use
circular synthesis with codec-period alignment; the rain is cyclically rotated
without discarding or fading samples. Twelve existing event sounds are retained.

The assets are supplied to the project owner for use, modification and
distribution with this project, including commercial game distribution.
No separate public/open-source asset license is granted here. Project ownership
and release policy govern redistribution outside this project. This provenance
notice does not assert that AI-generated material receives copyright protection
in every jurisdiction.
